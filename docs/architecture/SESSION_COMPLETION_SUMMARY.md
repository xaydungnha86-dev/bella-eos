## Key Innovation: The Thinking Layer

### Before Executive Capability:
```
CEO: "Tăng doanh thu 30%"
  ↓
(Black box - how did AI decide?)
  ↓
Creative Brief
  ↓
Banner
```

**Problem**: No strategic thinking. System jumps from intent to tactics.

### After Executive Capability:
```
CEO: "Tăng doanh thu 30%"
  ↓
Executive Capability (10 phases)
  1. Clarify goal → 1.5B in 4 weeks
  2. Diagnose → Retention broken
  3. Discover → 20 possibilities
  4. Constrain → Budget 150M
  5. Tradeoff → 3 options evaluated
  6. Reason → Balanced optimal
  7. Simulate → 80% confidence
  8. KPI → Full tree
  9. Allocate → 135M budget plan
  10. Approve → CEO decision request
  ↓
Executive Reasoning Package
  ↓
Decision Capability (tactical planning)
  ↓
Marketing OS (operationalization)
  ↓
Creative Capability (content creation)
  ↓
Banner (with full strategic traceability)
```

**Result**: Full transparency. Every action traces back to CEO goal.
